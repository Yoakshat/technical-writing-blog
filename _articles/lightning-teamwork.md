<p>
For the lightning talk presentation, my teammate and I communicated a day before the first round of presentations. This was a mistake. My teammate ended up choosing the document and creating a draft of the slides, because we were running out of time, and I didn't see his text. After Thursday, I added my own interpretation of the document and we decided to meet on Sunday, to split up speaking parts for the presentation. I learned that we should communicate earlier than we think we should, so we're both on the same page. If I had to do it again, I would discuss my interpretation of the artifact with my teammate and listen to his, and skillfully combine both. I would not split up this piece of work. In team writing tasks, I often suggest different perspectives my teammates don't think of. I am not usually the writer, or know how to word a sentence perfectly. However, I do provide a lot of ideas, and try to get my teammates to share what they think.
</p>
